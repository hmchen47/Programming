Author: Sachin Patil

## Basic Examples

- Example: simple Makefile
  - basic Makefile

    ```makefile
    say_hello:
      echo "Hello World"
    ```

  - executing `make` utility

    ```sh
    $ make
    echo "Hello World"
    Hello World
    ```
