## Example

- Metadata section
  - item type: [[Article]], [[Book]], [[Chapter]], [[Review]], [[Letter]], [[Photograph]], etc.
  - tags: `#literaturenote`, `#decade` (#2020s)
  - topics: relevant permanent notes and [maps of content](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/a-case-for-mocs/2418) (Types of Networks: centralized, distributed, decentralized)
  - example

    ```text
    # Example for Literatural Notes

    ---
    ## Meta

    Status:
    Item Type: [[Article]] #literaturenote
    Authors: [[2020]] #2020s
    Date Added: [[2020-07-07]]
    URL: https://martinezponciano.es/2021/04/05/from-fleeting-notes-to-literature-notes-obsidian/
    Topics: [[Obsidian]][[Academic writing]][[Template]][[literature notes]]
    ...
    ```


- Summary section
  - a couple of paragraphs summarising the main thesis/theses
  - the summary section will be expanded to include a more in-depth comparison to other scholarship in the field
  - example

    ```md
    Some text in which I cite an author.[^fn1]

    More text. Another citation.[^fn2]

    What is this? Yet _another_ citation?[^fn3]

    [^fn1]: [@krycho:revelation:2015], 6.
    [^fn2]: Contra [@krycho:revelation:2015], 15, who has everything _quite_ wrong.
    [^fn3]: [@krycho:revelation:2015].
    ```


- Key ideas section
  - the most important part of my literature note
  - the place
    - fleeting notes turned into critical summaries
    - discussions of a text by rearranging
    - merging, and condensing the fleeting notes
    - to get a theme-based summary of a text
  - a summary which discusses general ideas one at a time
  - literature notes shorter than my fleeting notes
  - every key idea discussed in a separate section
  - following a rigid naming convention: `Author (Year of Publication) AND description/backlink`
  - summarizing the key idea and/or providing important quotes from the text (using citekeys religiously) and using backlinks extensively
  - creating sections for every key idea
  - good literature notes $\to$ embedded in permanent notes
  - every single section and paragraph in my literature note is complete, unambiguous, and self-contained
  - refactoring key ideas into new files
    - alternative way for long one
    - creating much more solid backlinks which will not break if I change the title
    - making the notes easier to find through a simple search
  - example

    ```text
    ## Key ideas Markey (2011a)

    ### Anne Markey (2011a) and [[Oscar Wilde is not approached as a short story writer]]
    ### Anne Markey (2011a) and [[Oscar Wilde's short Fiction and Storytelling]] and [[Oscar Wilde's Short Fiction and Hermeneutics]]
    ...

    [[Anne Markey]][-@markey2011a, 74-75] points out that ...
    ```

- Differences fleeting notes and literature notes
  - fleeting notes:
    - those highlights ones
    - created on the source (PDF or paper)
    - stored in Zotero
  - literature notes
    - the rewritten versions of these notes
    - stored in Obsidian
  - enormous overlap between the two
  - transforming fleeting notes to literature notes: grouping all the highlights and notes of an article or book inevitably leads to summarizing the points and minimizing redundancy
